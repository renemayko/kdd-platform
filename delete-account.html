KDD Platform Account Deletion

KDD Platform allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open the KDD Platform listing on Google Play.
  2. Go to the App support or Developer contact section.
  3. Use the official support email listed there.
  4. Send the request from the email address associated with your KDD Platform account.
  5. Use the subject: KDD Account Deletion Request.
  6. Include the email address associated with the account and clearly state that you want the account deleted.

Data that may be deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, KDD Platform may delete account authentication information, profile information, email address, country information, verification status, and other personal information associated with the user account.

Data that may be retained

Certain records may be retained when necessary to comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute resolution requirements.

Any information that must be retained will be kept only for the period required for those purposes and will not be used for unrelated purposes.

Important

Users should never send passwords, authentication codes, government ID numbers, or other sensitive login credentials when requesting account deletion.
